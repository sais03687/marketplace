Release 2.11 - Pantry (recipe & meal planning app, iOS + Android)

#812 Shopping list: combine duplicate ingredients
  "2 eggs" from one recipe and "3 eggs" from another now show as "5 eggs". Different units (cups vs grams) are still listed separately.

#815 Fix crash when opening a recipe with no photo on Android 14
  Affected roughly 3% of Android sessions last week.

#817 Migrate image pipeline from Glide to Coil
  No visible change intended.

#819 Meal plan: copy last week's plan into this week
  One button on the week view. Doesn't copy the shopping list.

#820 Serving size slider now rounds to sensible amounts
  e.g. 1/3 cup instead of 0.333 cups; teaspoons round to nearest 1/4.

#821 Bump compileSdk to 35

#823 Fix: timers kept running after the recipe was closed, draining battery
  The notification now disappears when you leave the recipe.

#824 Analytics: add event for recipe_saved

#826 Nutrition panel
  Behind a feature flag, off for everyone. Not shipping in this release.

#828 Search understands "vegetarian", "vegan", "gluten-free" as filters
  Typing them in the search bar applies the filter instead of searching recipe titles for the word.

#830 Handle edge case in parser

#831 Offline mode: saved recipes open without a connection
  Only recipes saved after updating to 2.11; older saves need to be opened once online.
